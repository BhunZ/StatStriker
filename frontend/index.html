Prediction Models
TIER 1

Dixon-Coles

Classic Poisson model enhanced with attack/defense ratings per team and a rho correction for low-scoring draws. Time-weighted to emphasize recent form.

TIER 2

Bivariate Poisson

Models correlated goal counts between teams using a shared covariance parameter, capturing the tendency for matches to be jointly high or low scoring.

TIER 3

xG-Dixon-Coles

Replaces actual goals with expected goals (xG) from Understat, removing noise from lucky/unlucky finishes and measuring true chance creation.

TIER 4

Feature Poisson GLM

Generalized linear model with 8 curated feature stems: rolling xG, xPts, xG overperformance, goalkeeper save rate, goals-against rate, possession style, and points-per-match as predictors alongside team attack/defense ratings.

TIER 5 — ENSEMBLE

Stacked Ensemble

Weighted combination of all four models, optimized to minimize log-loss on temporal cross-validation folds. The final prediction used for all outputs.
